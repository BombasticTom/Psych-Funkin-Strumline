Animates a strumline into view by moving it from a vertical position.
Value 1: "add/remove [Strumline Name]".
Value 2: Animation settings, separated by semicolons ("Time; Tween; ID").
> Time – Duration measured in seconds (default: 2)
> Tween – Easing type (e.g. bouncein, sineinout) (default: linear)
[ADDITIONAL settings when ADDING your strumline]
> ID – Lane number where the strumline appears (default: center lane)
